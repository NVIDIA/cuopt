Where the divergence starts. Counting distinct iteration-0 primal objectives over each
batch of 10 runs isolates it to before the first factorization-driven step:

  2=SedumiMu             det on  |  1 distinct  (-3.701605598740e+02)
  2=SedumiMu             det off |  1 distinct  (-3.701605598740e+02)
  0=LustigMarstenShanno  det on  | 10 distinct  (-4.98837e-02, varying in the 8th digit)
  0=LustigMarstenShanno  det off | 10 distinct  (-4.98837e-02, varying in the 8th digit)

SedumiMu's starting point is a closed-form scalar formula, so it is bit-identical every
run and cuDSS deterministic mode is then enough to make the whole solve reproducible.
The LustigMarstenShanno and DualLeastSquares starting points are computed from a solve,
and that computation is nondeterministic at ~1e-8 relative regardless of the cuDSS
determinism flag. The flag cannot recover reproducibility for them because the runs have
already diverged at iteration 0; it only changes the factorization numerics downstream,
which on this model happens to spread the iteration counts further and pushes 2 of 10
runs into a soft breakdown.
