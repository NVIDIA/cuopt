Two effects, the second more important than the one we went looking for.

1. Barrier time on the deterministic path improves by 6 to 16 percent, mostly by
   needing fewer iterations rather than by a cheaper matvec:

     SedumiMu             2388 -> 2013 ms  (-16%)   29 -> 24 iterations
     LustigMarstenShanno  2564 -> 2202 ms  (-14%)        -> 23 iterations
     DualLeastSquares     2135 -> 2006 ms   (-6%)        -> 24 iterations

   The determinism-off cells barely move (237->227, 287->273, 249->229 ms), so the
   matvec itself was not a large share of a non-deterministic solve.

2. All three starting points become reproducible under cuDSS determinism, not just
   SedumiMu. Replicated in an independent batch (../repeat10_strategy_det_csrir_rep2):
   LustigMarstenShanno 10/10 at 23 iterations bit-identical, DualLeastSquares 10/10 at
   24 iterations bit-identical.

   This confirms the mechanism diagnosed earlier from the iteration-0 objectives. The
   LustigMarstenShanno and DualLeastSquares starting points are computed by a solve that
   runs iterative refinement, and that refinement's matvec was the matrix-free
   augmented_multiply, which is not deterministic. Counting distinct iteration-0 primal
   objectives over each batch of 10 runs:

     LustigMarstenShanno, det on, csr_ir_matvec=0 : 10 distinct
     LustigMarstenShanno, det on, csr_ir_matvec=1 :  1 distinct

   So cuDSS determinism alone could never fix these two: the runs had already diverged
   before the first factorization. Routing the refinement matvec through cuSPARSE ALG2
   closes the last non-deterministic operation in the path.

Accuracy. The objective each strategy settles on under determinism:

     LustigMarstenShanno  -0.098508019031012944
     DualLeastSquares     -0.098508019055016049
     SedumiMu             -0.098508015995420889

   The first two agree to 2.4e-11. SedumiMu lands on the other of the two attractors we
   saw earlier, 3.06e-09 away. All three are inside the requested 1e-8 tolerance, and
   all are within 2.2e-9 of the failure-free step-0.99 reference (-0.098508018210).
