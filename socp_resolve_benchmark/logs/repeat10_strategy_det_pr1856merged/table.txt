The conclusion is unchanged: SedumiMu + cuDSS determinism is the only reproducible
combination, and determinism does not help the other two starting points.

Only one cell is exactly comparable between the two builds, since the other five are
nondeterministic by construction and resample a different trajectory each time.
That cell agrees bit for bit:

  SedumiMu, det on   pre-merge  29 iters  -0.098508019055644866
  SedumiMu, det on  post-merge  29 iters  -0.098508019055644866

The differences in the nondeterministic cells (SedumiMu det off picking up 1 soft
failure and a wider 1.49e-07 spread, DualLeastSquares det on going from 2 to 3 soft
failures) are resampling noise, not an effect of the merge. The pre-merge replicate
batches already spanned that range: LustigMarstenShanno det on gave 10/10 optimal in
one batch and 8/10 in the next, on the same build. On this 5103x10102 matrix the
merged predicate alg2_beta_bug_possible is false, so both builds select ALG2 for every
SpMV, which is why the reproducible cell matches exactly.
